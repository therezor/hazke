Space sim for the M5Stack Cardputer

HAZKE

Trade. Hunt. Survive.

Fly your own ship through sixteen star systems on a pocket computer. Buy cargo where it's cheap and sell it where it isn't, take jobs from planets, and fight the pirates who want what's in your hold.

How it plays

Earn credits, upgrade your ship, go further.

Trade

Every system has its own market. Prices depend on the local economy, the government and your reputation.

Fight

Lasers, homing missiles and an ECM. Pirates attack on sight, and a ship you beat can be looted.

Take jobs

Planets post contracts: hunt raiders, deliver cargo, carry passengers. The pay grows the further from home you go.

Explore

Jump gates link sixteen star systems. Each has its own planets, government and economy.

Get started

You need a Cardputer and a USB-C cable.

  1. InstallPick Hazke in your launcher's app list, or download the firmware and flash it with M5Burner or esptool.py --chip esp32s3 write_flash 0x0 hazke-v1.3.0-cardputer.bin. Upgrading? Copy your saves to the SD card first. A full flash clears the ones in internal memory.
  2. StartChoose NEW GAME and type your name. You start next to a jump gate with 100 credits.
  3. FlyFly into a planet to land and trade. Fly into a jump gate to travel to another system.
