What's in the cockpit

A full space sim, on a device that fits your palm.

Every system below is grown from a single seed — the same galaxy, the same prices, the same pirates, every boot. Original code, original universe.

Exploration

A Living Galaxy

Sixteen star systems wired together by jump gates — each with its own economy, government, tech level and population. Fly into a gate to open the chart and pick your next destination.

Flight

Free-Flight Cockpit

Real 3D space with coordinated banking turns. Shaded polygon planets, ringed worlds, asteroid belts and a sun hot enough to cook your hull — all rendered live on the handheld.

Economy

Make Your Fortune

Seventeen commodities priced by supply, local law and your reputation. Buy low under one government, run narcotics past another, sell high one gate over.

Combat

Pick a Fight

Pulse, beam and military lasers. Up to four homing missiles with target lock, regenerating shields, and an ECM blast that scatters incoming fire before it lands.

Missions

Take Contracts

Patrol, delivery, courier, recon and scavenge jobs off every planet's board — scaled in danger and pay by how far from home they send you.

Progression

Earn Your Rank

Four factions track every kill and trade. Climb seven combat ranks from Harmless to Deadly — and sour the wrong faction, and its patrols start hunting you on sight.

Docking is a touch.

There's no docking mini-game. Drift up against any planet and you land automatically — opening its market, equipment shop, mission board and status screen.

Gates are the only way out.

Fly physically into a system's jump gate and the galaxy chart opens. Pick a neighbouring system, confirm, and ride witchspace across — jumps cost credits by distance.

The sun bites.

Stray too deep into a star's corona and the HEAT warning climbs. Push past it and your hull cooks — fly into the photosphere and you're gone instantly.

Hail before you holster.

Traders are always open for business. Pirates and patrols only talk once you've shot their hull below 25% — your call whether that's mercy or salvage.

What you need to get flying.

Hazke targets one specific handheld. If you have a Cardputer, you're three steps from the gate.

Requirements

  • ▸M5Stack Cardputer — ESP32-S3 with a built-in keyboard and a 1.14″ ST7789 screen.
  • ▸A USB-C cable to flash it from your computer.
  • ▸A launcher or Arduino IDE — load the prebuilt firmware, or open hazke.ino and upload.
  • ▸Nothing online. The whole universe lives on the chip; every run starts a fresh commander.

Install in three

  1. Get the firmwareGrab Hazke from your launcher's app catalog, or build hazke.ino in the Arduino IDE with the board set to M5Cardputer.
  2. Flash over USB-CConnect the Cardputer and upload. It boots straight to the title screen.
  3. NEW GAMEArrow to NEW GAME, press ENTER, and you'll wake at the jump gate of System 00 — the safe starter system. Go make some credits.
